Buying Estate Land in Nigeria — FAQs

Which cities does Jecyani Properties sell land in?

We currently offer verified estate land in Abuja, Umuahia, Aba, Asaba and Enugu, with expansion underway to Lagos and Port Harcourt. Every listing on this page is government-approved and fully documented.

Can I compare estate prices before choosing a plot?

Yes. Every estate card above shows its price range, location and included features up front, so you can compare gated communities across cities before speaking with our team.

Do all estates offer flexible payment plans?

Yes. Every estate listed here supports our 30% deposit installment plan, with the balance spread over 3, 6, or 12 months and no hidden charges.

What documents come with each plot?

Every plot includes a registered survey plan and Irrevocable Power of Attorney at minimum; several estates also include a Deed of Conveyance or full Certificate of Occupancy (C of O) — see each estate's detail page for its exact documentation package.

What's the difference between shell finish and finished units?
Shell finish units come with the structural work completed — walls, roof, windows and basic fittings — ready for you to complete the interior to your taste at your own pace. Finished units are move-in ready with full interior finishing including kitchen, bathrooms and wardrobes. Shell finish typically saves you ₦55M–₦78M upfront.

Yes. Port Harcourt is one of Nigeria's wealthiest cities due to the oil and gas industry. The presence of multinational companies creates consistent demand for quality residential properties, driving strong rental yields and capital appreciation for investors.
